# Flashcards - Day 001

**Q1. What are the steps of the CPU's basic cycle?**
A: Fetch, decode, execute (then store result and repeat).

**Q2. Why is RAM called volatile?**
A: It loses all its contents when power is removed.

**Q3. Why are programs loaded from disk into RAM before running?**
A: The disk is far too slow for the CPU; RAM is much faster.

**Q4. What does POST stand for and what does it do?**
A: Power-On Self Test - firmware checks that CPU, RAM and basic hardware work before booting.

**Q5. Where does BIOS look for boot code?**
A: In the MBR, the first 512 bytes of the boot disk.

**Q6. What does UEFI load instead of an MBR?**
A: A `.efi` bootloader from the EFI System Partition (FAT32).

**Q7. What is the job of the bootloader?**
A: Load the OS kernel into RAM (and optionally let you choose an OS).

**Q8. What is Secure Boot?**
A: A UEFI feature that only runs boot components with a trusted digital signature.

**Q9. Why are bootkits dangerous?**
A: They run before the OS, so they survive reinstalls and can hide from the OS and antivirus.

**Q10. What is the difference between MBR and GPT?**
A: MBR: older, max 2 TB, 4 primary partitions. GPT: modern, huge disks, many partitions, used by UEFI.
