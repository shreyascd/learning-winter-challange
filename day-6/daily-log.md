## Day 006 - Binary, hex and encodings (ASCII/UTF-8)
**Topic:** Binary, hex and encodings (ASCII/UTF-8)

### 3 things learned
1. One byte is 8 bits (0-255), and one hex digit is exactly 4 bits, so two hex digits make a byte.
2. ASCII is 7-bit, and UTF-8 keeps ASCII as single bytes while using 2-4 bytes for other characters.
3. Base64 and URL encoding are encoding, not encryption, so they hide nothing from an analyst.
